Glossary

One word per concept, one concept per word. Every public name in the loops crate appears here, and every word here means one thing across the page and the code. Plain words win; vertex is the one word kept from the strange-loop vocabulary, on purpose.

Records

TermMeans
recordThe unit of everything: an envelope, a payload and a signature, named by a content hash. An observation, never a statement of truth.
emitTo write a record. One of the two write verbs; the other is tick.
envelopeThe fields the core reads: kind, observer, emitted, occurred, about, prev, under. A field belongs here only if the core reads it.
payloadEverything else. The core never interprets it; folds read typed fields from it.
kindA record's label. It stays a label, not a type; declarations describe it.
observerWhoever says "I saw": a lineage id on every record. A collector is an observer reporting hearsay.
emitted · occurredTwo times. emitted is when the observer wrote it; occurred is the claimed span, and may be coarse.
aboutThe things a record is about: a set, usually one.
prevThe records this one replaces, within the things both are about: a set. Empty starts a chain; two or more merge. It may name only records the writer holds about a shared thing.
underThe grant a record relies on.
commitment · saltThe payload's hash, salted so a withheld payload can't be guessed. Opening it means showing the payload and the salt.
erasureRemoving a payload but keeping its commitment, so the id and every tick that cites it still hold.

Things and identity

TermMeans
thingWhatever records are about: a chapter, a service, a URL, a person. Each thing has its own state; a record about nothing lives in one thing of its own.
anchorThe record a thing or a lineage starts with; its id is the identity. Primitives start with the reserved *-anchor kinds.
natural keyA typed key that needs no anchor, such as url:, isbn: or svc:. Anyone computes the same one.
birth keyThe natural key for code: the symbol as it was in the commit that created it.
lineageA stable identity under changing names and keys: the hash of its anchor. Observers and stores are lineages; a vertex is named by its spec's hash instead.
name · name table · @homeNames resolve at read through name tables and never take part in matching. @home is the table your realm starts from.
same-as · disputeThe two identity kinds: these are one thing, or they are not.
resolve-byHow a vertex joins identities: it folds same-as and dispute records into classes.
facetOne kind about one thing, emitted whole; the current state is the latest per kind.

Meaning

TermMeans
storeA set of records with its own anchor and declarations. It holds records; it owns no meaning.
declaration · maturityRecords in a store describing its kinds, writers and policy. A store matures from open to declared to strict.
vertexA standing question over stores: it selects, folds and ticks, and its ticks feed higher vertices. Where loops meet, which nothing else names, so it keeps its word from the original vocabulary.
specA vertex's definition: inputs, folds, a boundary. One spec can be instantiated as several vertices, each named by the hash of the spec and its parameters. Written as JSON, stored as a record of kind vertex; its note explains it and stays out of the input key.
foldTurning a set of records into state, with one of six: Count, Sum, Min, Max, LatestBy, Collect. Accumulating folds are commutative; folds over heads follow prev. Latest, TopN and Window are modifiers of these, and Avg is left to the lens.
headA record nothing about the same thing names in prev: a thing's current record. A thing with several heads is forked, shown as a conflict. Heads are worked out from envelopes alone, over everything the reader holds.
content orderHow a fold decides which record wins: taken from what records carry, never from arrival. No fold needs records read in this order.
predicateWherever a spec says which records, or when to tick. A closed, typed set.
boundary · markerWhen a vertex ticks. A marker is a record whose arrival is that moment.
tickA vertex's result at a moment, covering exactly the records it folded. Also the verb: a vertex ticks at each boundary. A content tick is a value anyone can recompute, with an id over its vertex, covers and result; it is stored, when cited or sent, as a record of kind tick signed by its writer. A witness tick is such a record saying what the runner had.
coversThe records a tick folded, listed exactly; also its field, covers=(…).
as ofThe cut a fold is taken at, for either time. Always an input, never the clock.

Standing

TermMeans
standingWhether a record takes effect, given who signed it.
admissionThe standing check, made at read: a record without standing stays visible but has no effect.
stanceA record that affirms, adopts, disputes, retires or authorizes another.
stewardAn observer with standing over a store's declarations and policy.
grantStanding given ahead of time, with a scope. Agents act within it.
presenceA person present, signing with a key behind Touch ID or similar, for acts that can't be undone. A grant is presence given ahead of time.
seatAn identity shared by delegates who don't have their own yet.

Places and sharing

TermMeans
placeWhere a store's records live on a machine: a location, a format and a role.
segmentOne writer's file of records in a place. Closed once committed; named by hash.
realmThe stores reachable from one root name table. Realms nest by linking, never by rewriting.
promotionCopying a record, verbatim, into another store in your realm.
bridgeCrossing into another realm: always explicit, always a signed tick.
bundleA tick sent together with everything it covers.
changesetRecords emitted together as one transaction, as a lens apply does.
collectorAn external process that turns a source into records, over stdin.

Reading out

TermMeans
lensA two-way transform between state and a file: render for an audience, parse edits back, diff by ids, apply as records. Absence is never an edit.
baseWhat a render came from: tick, lens hash and audience. Edits are diffed against it.
audienceWho a render is for; it decides what is withheld.
input key · result hashA result's two names: what went in, and what came out.

API words

TermMeans
RealmPackage loops's handle on the stores you can read. Reads span all of them; writes name one store. Narrow restricts it to a grant.
DraftA record before it is signed.
StateWhat a fold returns.
AsOfThe cut, for both times.
CheckpointA place's segment list as last seen. What's new since it is how watching works. Not "cursor": the editor already owns that word.
SignerThe trait for anything that signs: a device key, the keychain, a file.
PlaceThe trait for storage: list, get by id, what's new since a checkpoint, a blob by hash. loops-dir is the one built in.
LensThe trait for render and parse; the core does the diff.
RefusalAn error with a code from a closed set, and the field it concerns.
