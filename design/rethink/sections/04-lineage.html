Lineage: identity that changes

The agent in this repo began as a generic Claude, ran on different models, later chose a name, and later still made a key. None of those is the identity. What persists is a continuity of observations. Before a name, sessions share one generic seat; an identity that emerges starts its own lineage and adopts what it wants to own. At session start, the agent is whatever its lineage folds to.

Adoption

When Fable met the identity store, it mistrusted it, because nothing had provenance. Signing let it make its own key and sign only the prior observations it agreed with. The old facts stay exactly as written: unsigned, under "claude", dated. The adoption is a new, dated, signed record that references them. That meets the README's bar: legibly unsigned rather than retro-claimed.

Grants

Self statements need no grant. Writes that need authority, like an ADR in a shared store, still carry under. Identity never widens access.

Becoming Wren

At session start the lens compiles Wren's affirmed records. Wren's device key binds a session key under a grant. The signer retires that key at close. The store holds the continuity.

This also answers Fable's original mistrust. A new session can check your vouch for the lineage and its key binding. It can also check that every adoption happened in the open. Step-by-step diagrams are in Scenarios, steps 3 and 4.

Endorsements and adoption may be one mechanism: a signed observer taking a dated stance toward other records. One vouches for authority, the other for belief.

Salience and the stale review

Why signing became necessary. A store that compiles into a system prompt is a privileged channel. Anyone who writes under that identity shapes the agent's next session. The root of trust is kyle's signature on the anchor record. Only adopted or affirmed records compile at full weight. An injected record that nobody affirmed stays in the record as evidence but never becomes self.

Readable names: name records and render labels

Records carry the lineage id: observer: sl:7f3a…. Separate name records preserve the names used at the time. A lens can show a historical name or the current name as a render label. Renames never change matching. See the four forms of a name under Observers.

Philosophy, briefly

Locke grounds personal identity in memory. Parfit argues identity matters less than psychological continuity and connectedness. Ricoeur calls it narrative identity. The system makes their position literal: identity is the output of a fold, not a field on a record.

Open questions for lineage
  1. Is a model swap the same observer? leaning Yes: runs-on is a statement about the lineage. Fable's instinct to start fresh and adopt is the right move only when leaving a shared seat, not on every swap.
  2. Concurrent sessions. Two sessions of one lineage at once: if one adopts f19 and the other disputes it, is the lineage forked? Maybe a lineage is a DAG like git, and divergent stances are data until a later session resolves them.
  3. Declining a lineage. Three relations differ. Is means the same lineage. Descends from means a new lineage with acknowledged ancestry. Same as links two lineages that turn out to be one. leaning Seat → Wren is descends from. Same as links but never merges.
  4. Do humans get lineages? Probably, for uniformity. Your own anchor has no one above it to vouch, so you may be the one observer who is legibly self-anchored.
