The conversation, turn by turn

1 · Rewrite in another language?

kyle

Consider rewriting from scratch for a cleaner, faster implementation. Back-compat not needed; a clean, portable, easy-to-install core is.

claude

  • About 77k source and 115k test lines; the model is small and the folds are a closed vocabulary.
  • Conformance vectors already anticipate another implementation.
  • Ledger: costs are algorithmic; a language fixes cold start, not replay-on-open.
  • Lean Rust, Go credible. Expand vectors first, core library before CLI, guard against re-accretion.
2 · Arrival got out of hand

kyle

The early CLI was workable for agents emitting during work. Arrival began from sharing repo knowledge at work (process, history, decisions up to ADRs) via git or JSONL, and tried to be mergeable by default. The library is the product.

claude

  • Single-writer custody vs a multi-writer problem; receipt order breaks convergence.
  • Merge as set union; fold on content order with checkpoints.
  • Let git handle custody for the team case; per-observer JSONL; SQLite as a disposable index; facts reviewed in PRs.
3 · The full topology

kyle

Hand-built stores (a novel, an auth RFC), ingestion stores (newsletters, RSS, subreddits, likes, Safari history), composition into a salience feed, personal-life stores, and ticks in agent orchestration with lossless fidelity. Arrival tried to be git-storable without being git-only.

claude

  • Three relationships: compose, bridge, replicate. Most stores have one writer.
  • Immutable content-addressed facts and blobs; single-writer segments; derived index.
  • Composition makes content order mandatory. Ticks as content-addressed seals; references as a privacy boundary.
