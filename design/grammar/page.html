A record is one sentence

The envelope is the grammar of a single observation: who saw, what sort of thing they saw, of what, when, and what it replaces. The core reads every word of it and nothing else.

The payload is the sentence's content. The core never interprets it, so it can be withheld or erased and the sentence still parses: its id, its place in the chain and every test on its envelope hold.

Nouns

Each noun has one purpose, its own state, and an operating principle: what happens, step by step, when you use it. The laws under each card are the theorems that hold it to its principle.

Verbs

Two verbs write: emit and tick. Every other verb reads, and reading never changes a store. A lens closes the loop the only way anything gets down to the ground: its edits become records.

Most of what feels like an action is a construction, an emit with a field filled in. Revising, merging and erasing are sentences, not new verbs.

A closed set: every code the vectors pin is here, and the build fails if one isn't.

Layers

Six layers, each as high as it is temporary. The ground is what was said; nothing on it is ever edited. Each layer above is worked out from the one below it, so any of them can be dropped and rebuilt.

Every arrow up recomputes. The only arrow down is a record.

A walk

One decision, nine steps: two people write, fork, merge and erase; a reader with less sees the same; a vertex ticks; a lens turns the result into text and an edit back into a record.
